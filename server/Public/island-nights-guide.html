Welcome! This is everything you need to add your own photos, video, and details for your upcoming Island Nights date — no restaurant staff needed once you're set up.

  1. Log in

    Go to ohanasushigrill.com/login and sign in with the username and temporary password above.

  2. Open Island Nights

    Once you're logged in, you'll land on a page with an Island Nights button — tap it. That's the only tool your account can see.

  3. Add your date, time, and name

    Tap + Add Performer, pick the Wednesday you're playing, set your start time (defaults to 9pm), and enter your name (or your act's name). You can only book dates within the next 60 days — if a date doesn't save, that's likely why.

  4. Add a caption

    Use the Bio / note field for a short line about your set — this is what shows on the website next to your name.

  5. Add photos

    Tap + Add Photo to upload one or more pictures. These show up right on the Entertainment page for guests to see before your night.

  6. Add a video (with sound!)

    Tap + Add Video and choose a clip from your phone or computer. Make sure the sound is on in the clip itself — it plays with audio on the website, so a silent video won't show off your set the way you want it to.

  7. Save

    Tap Save at the bottom of your entry. You can come back and edit it anytime before your date — add more photos, swap the video, fix a typo.
