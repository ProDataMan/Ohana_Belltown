Table Order: Wait, Prep & Delivery Times

From real completed table orders — wait is order placed → staff entered it, prep is entered → delivered, total is placed → delivered. Items with too few completed orders to average are left off the list, but still count toward the overall averages.

Estimated Table Occupancy

How long a table is typically occupied, seating to departure — there's no way to measure this directly (nothing tracks when a party actually leaves), so it combines one real measurement with two research-grounded estimates: the real average wait+prep time for that table's own completed orders above, plus an eating-time estimate based on what was ordered (a full entree lingers longer than a shared appetizer or a sipped drink — the single slowest dish, or half the combined total, whichever is greater), plus a fixed allowance for deciding what to order and for conversation/paying/leaving afterward, sized to land within the 60–90 minute range industry research reports for casual full-service dining.
