Weekly hours

A closing time at or before the opening time means after midnight (open 4:30 PM, close 1:00 AM). "Kitchen closes" is when food orders stop; leave it blank if the kitchen is open until close.

Holidays & special days

A date here replaces that day's regular hours (for example, closed for Thanksgiving). Past dates drop off automatically.
