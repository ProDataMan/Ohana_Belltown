Table Orders

Guests tap "Order" next to a menu item after scanning their table's QR code — this isn't routed through ChowNow or any payment system yet, just a heads-up to go check with the table and enter it. Tap Confirm Entered once you've actually entered it into the order system — the order then moves to "Awaiting Delivery" until someone taps Confirm Delivered (a guest can also confirm it themselves from the menu page). That full order-to-delivery time feeds the prep-time estimates and analytics.
