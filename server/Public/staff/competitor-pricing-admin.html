Compares our menu prices against nearby restaurants for similar dishes, so pricing stays in line with the neighborhood instead of guessing. This isn't a live feed — there's no reliable API for a competitor's actual published prices — so it's only as current as the last time someone here checked a competitor's menu/site/delivery app and entered it below.

How to Add a Comparison

  1. Find a restaurant. In "Competitor Restaurants" below, click Find Nearby Restaurants and add any worth comparing against — no need to type a name or address.
  2. Create a comparison group. In "Comparison Groups," name the dish you're comparing (e.g. "Ohana Burger" or a generic "Classic Cheeseburger") — link it to one of our own menu items if there's a direct match, so our side of the comparison always shows the live current price.
  3. Look up the competitor's price. Check the restaurant's own site, a menu photo you've uploaded (see "Menu Photos" in Competitor Restaurants), or their delivery-app listing for what they charge for that dish.
  4. Log it as a Price Entry. In "Price Entries," pick the group and restaurant, enter their price, and optionally a source link/date. Add one entry per competitor for the same group to build out the comparison.
  5. Check the report. The "Price Comparison Report" below updates automatically — it shows our price alongside the competitor average/min/max and how far off we are.
