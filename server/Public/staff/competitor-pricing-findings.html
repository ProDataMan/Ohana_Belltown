Caveats on This Data

  • Sample sizes are thin. Only Ohana Burger (n=8), Chicken Wings (n=5), French Fries (n=5), Gyoza (n=3) and a handful of n=2 groups have more than one competitor price behind them. Everything else is a single restaurant's menu on a single day.
  • Ludi's Restaurant's menu photo is dated April 2024 — over two years old. If their prices have gone up since, the Ohana Burger and Loco Moco gaps against them are actually smaller than shown.
  • Cyclops and The 5 Point Cafe both had multiple, conflicting price sheets (an older printed menu vs. current at Cyclops; separate Lunch & Dinner vs. Brunch & Late Night sheets at 5 Point). The higher/more-current sheet was used in both cases — if that was the wrong call, those two burger entries run a little high.
  • Distance is staff-estimated for all 13 restaurants added, not Google-verified — the Nearby Search picker never surfaced any of them (likely its ~20-result cap, or category filtering that excludes some bars/pop-ups from a straight "restaurant" search). Doesn't affect the prices themselves, just how "nearby" each comparison really is.
