Staff Rewards

100 points ≈ $1 of reward value.

A points card for keeping the site itself up to date — points are scaled to effort: marking a special or updating a price is worth 100 points, adding a photo or an event is worth 200, and a social media post is worth 300. Editing a menu item auto-awards the relevant points; adding an event does too. Staff can log unverifiable "other" activity from My Account instantly, but a social media post needs a link and goes to the Social Media Requests queue below for approval first — those points aren't credited until you approve it.

Points are redeemed from the Reward Catalog below — food items are priced against the Happy Hour menu; swag is listed but not yet redeemable until it has a real point cost.
