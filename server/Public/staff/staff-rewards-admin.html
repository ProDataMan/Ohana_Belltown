Staff Rewards

A points card for keeping the site itself up to date — points are scaled to effort: marking a special or updating a price is worth 1 point, adding a photo or an event is worth 2, and a social media post is worth 3 (roughly $1 of reward per point). Editing a menu item auto-awards the relevant points; adding an event does too. Staff can log their own unverifiable activity (like a social post) from My Account, or award points by hand below for anything else worth recognizing.

10 points = one free Classic Ohana Roll or Happy Hour appetizer (see the Happy Hour menu) — redeem it below once a staff member gets there.
