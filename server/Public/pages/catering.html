Catering & Private Events

Let us bring the Aloha to your event. Whether it's an office lunch, a wedding, a birthday, or just a gathering that deserves great food, our catering trays bring the same Hawaiian-Japanese flavors you'd get in the restaurant straight to wherever you're celebrating — hand-rolled sushi platters, poke, and pupu's, built to order and sized for your headcount. No neon sign or tiki decor required (though we can't promise you won't miss it).

Off-Site Catering

Interested in having some Aloha at your next big event? Let Ohana's cater your celebration. We specialize in weddings, birthdays, and private & work occasions, and offer catering packages for any size of event.

Feel free to contact us and get in touch with our Catering & Special Events Manager.
