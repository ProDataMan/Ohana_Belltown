Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take reservations?

Walk-ins are always welcome. For larger parties or special occasions, call or text us ahead and we'll get a table ready. You can also join our waitlist from your phone before you arrive.

What are your hours?

Hours can shift around holidays and events, so the most reliable place to check is our Google listing or a quick call to (206) 956-9329. Happy Hour runs Monday–Friday, 3-6pm — see the Happy Hour menu.

Where do I park?

We're in Belltown at 2207 1st Avenue — street parking and nearby paid lots/garages are the norm for the neighborhood. Give yourself a few extra minutes if you're driving in on a busy night.

Do you have vegetarian, gluten-free, or allergen information?

Yes — our menu has filter chips for common dietary needs and allergens (vegetarian, gluten-free-available, shellfish, dairy, and more) right on each item. If you have a serious allergy, it's always worth confirming with your server directly too.

Do you do takeout or delivery?

Yes, through ChowNow — order online here.

Can you host a private party or large group?

Yes — see our Catering & Private Events page for booking Ohana's own space, or having us cater your event elsewhere.

Is there a rewards program?

Yes, our sushi punch card — order sushi, earn punches, and 10 punches gets you a free roll. Sharing a photo or tagging us on social also earns a small bonus. See Rewards for the details.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes — buy one online, any amount from $5–$500.

How long has Ohana been around?

Since 1999 — we're the oldest Tiki & Sushi Bar in Washington. Read more on our About page.

Don't see your question here? Contact us and we'll get you an answer.
