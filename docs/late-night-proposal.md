# Late Night Aloha: a Friday & Saturday late-night deal

*From the website's QR menu data, Nov 7–21, 2026*

## The short version

Almost half of the people scanning our table QR codes do it **after 10pm, mostly on Friday and Saturday nights**. Many of them reach for the Happy Hour card, looking for a deal that isn't on. A late-night deal built around drinks and a few easy bites would meet them where they already are, without needing the kitchen to stay late.

## What the data shows

- **46% of QR visits are late night.** 38 of 83 guest visits (not counting staff testing) started between 10pm and 2am.
- **It's a weekend thing.** 32 of those 38 were Friday or Saturday nights. The rest of the week barely registers after 10pm.
- **Peak is 11pm.** 11pm–midnight was the busiest hour of the whole day for QR use (17 visits), more than any dinner hour.
- **Late-night guests are deal-hunting.** 11 late-night visits came from flipping the table card to the **Happy Hour** side, and 14 opened the Happy Hour menu. Happy Hour is 3–6pm, so none of those prices applied.
- **They're in the dining room, not the bar.** Late scans came from tables 24, 30, 44, 47, 48, 26 and 42: groups sitting down, not just bar seats.

What late-night guests looked at most:

- Fries (opened 3 times, photo tapped 3 times, and searched for by name)
- California Roll, salmon rolls and nigiri, Spicy Tuna Roll
- Blue Hawaiian, Mango Madness, Lychee Martini
- Fish Tacos, Hawaiian Style Poke, Takoyaki
- Spicy Teriyaki Wings, Panko Popper, Edamame (photo taps)

## The proposal

**"Late Night Aloha," Friday & Saturday, 10pm to close.**

Lead with drinks, because they carry the margin and need no kitchen. Add a short bites list that the people still on shift can serve.

### Drinks (the deal)

- **Blue Hawaiian** and **Lychee Martini**: these are our most profitable drinks and already the ones late-night guests open. A late-night price on these two is the headline.
- **Draft beer** and **Twisted Tea**: easy add-ons for groups.
- **Mango Madness**: it's getting looked at late at night, so it's worth including if the blender's still on.

A reasonable starting point is matching Happy Hour drink prices (Blue Hawaiian is $14 at Happy Hour vs $16 regular, Lychee Martini $14 vs $15), since guests are already looking for those.

### Bites: matched to who's still working

The kitchen line leaves at 10–11pm on weekends, so this is in three tiers. Pick the tier that matches who's actually in the building.

**Tier 1: no cook needed (made ahead, bar can serve)**
- **Spam Musubi**: made before close and wrapped, it holds well. It's the classic Hawaiian late-night snack.
- **Hawaiian Style Poke**: cold, pre-marinated.
- **Edamame**: steamed ahead, served chilled with salt.
- **Seaweed Salad**: cold, plated.

**Tier 2: if the sushi bar stays until midnight**
- **California Roll**, **Spicy Tuna Roll**, **Salmon Roll**: the most-opened late-night items, simple to roll, and good margin.

**Tier 3: if one person can run the fryer until close**
- **Fries**: the single most-looked-at late-night item.
- **Takoyaki**, **Spicy Teriyaki Wings**: from frozen or pre-cooked, finished in the fryer.

If only Tier 1 is possible, it still works: the drinks are the deal, and the bites are there so groups stay for another round.

## Why it's worth trying

- **The demand already shows up.** We're not trying to create late-night traffic. Friday and Saturday 10pm–1am is already our busiest QR window.
- **It turns a disappointment into a sale.** Right now late-night guests flip to Happy Hour and find prices that don't apply. The website now explains that and sends them to the full menu, but a real late-night offer would give them what they were looking for.
- **Low cost to run.** It's drinks first, with bites that need no line cook. No new staffing is needed for Tier 1.
- **It pushes our best-margin drinks.** Blue Hawaiian and Lychee Martini are already featured in the QR "Try our…" popup. Late night is when the most people see it.

## How we'll know if it works

The website can track this from day one:

- **QR visits by hour** on Fridays and Saturdays, before vs after launch (QR Insights → "When people scan").
- **Popup results**: how often "Try our Blue Hawaiian / Lychee Martini" gets tapped late at night.
- **What actually sells**: once Heartland ticket data is connected, we can see late-night orders by table and compare them with what guests looked at.

If we launch it, the website can also send the Happy Hour table-card side to a **Late Night** menu after 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays, and feature the late-night drinks in the popup during those hours.

## Caveats

- This is two weeks of data, and only from guests who use the QR menu, not everyone in the room.
- We can see what people **look at**, not yet what they **order**. The Heartland connection will fill that in.
- Fryer and sushi-bar hours are your call. The tiers are there so this works with whoever is on shift.
